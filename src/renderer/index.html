How BLSS Trainer works
  1. Connect your controller, then calibrate the left stick by pushing up, down, left, and right.
  2. Calibrate the B button by clicking the calibration button and pressing B on your controller.
  3. Choose a session mode. Timer mode starts after the countdown. Hold B mode starts when you press B and stops when you release it.
  4. During a run, keep B held and keep the left stick moving. Releasing B or leaving the stick centered too long stops the attempt.
  5. Use the heatmap, center score, rhythm score, and frequency to understand what to improve.
